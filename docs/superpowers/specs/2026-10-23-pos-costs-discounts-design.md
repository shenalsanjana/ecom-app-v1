# POS costs and discounts

Support one additional cost and one discount per cart line and on the entire bill. Each accepts fixed LKR (integer cents) or percentages (integer hundredths of a percent). Fixed item amounts apply to the entire quantity line. Apply the cost first, then the discount at each level; bill percentages apply after item adjustments and exclude delivery, as confirmed by the user. Keep the existing free-delivery policy based on original product subtotal.

Share integer-cent calculations between client and server. Reject invalid precision, negative/over-limit values, discounts beyond their base, and unsafe totals. Permit fully discounted cash sales with zero received; non-cash zero totals are rejected. Preserve old pending hashes with optional, default-free adjustment inputs. Persist signed computed amounts and descriptive scope/rate/SKU labels in existing OrderAdjustment rows atomically with stock/order creation. Freeze these saved price agreements against item/adjustment edits in Orders; delivery contact/address and lifecycle controls remain available for COD. No migration. Test arithmetic, retries, payments, snapshots, receipt/order reconciliation and mobile UX under hard resource limits; deploy app-only with rollback.
