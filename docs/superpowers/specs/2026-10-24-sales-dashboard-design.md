# Sales dashboard

The existing admin dashboard has operational counts only. Orders already unify ONLINE/POS, payment status, items, adjustments and gateway events. No product costs or structured refunds are recorded. Reuse those sources and preserve checkout, gateway, inventory and cancellation behavior.

Report revenue by payment-received date (confirmed by the user) in Asia/Colombo; refunds use their verified refund date. Count each order once, never gateway events. PAID/COD_COLLECTED count as settled; pending/failed payments do not contribute revenue. Exclude cancelled orders from sales and profit. Show all recorded refunds, with cancelled-order refunds separately identified and excluded from sales deductions to avoid subtracting twice. Net sales excludes delivery; revenue includes net delivery. Gross profit is net merchandise sales less product cost, with explicitly recorded resellable returns recovering their saved cost. Refund recording records payments already completed outside the app; it does not send funds or alter inventory. Return quantities are permitted only for dispatched/delivered orders and mean units already returned to stock.

Add optional product cost in integer cents, order-line cost/list-price/product/category snapshots, settlement timestamp/legacy estimate marker, structured adjustment SKU attribution, and an audited/idempotent refund ledger. Costs are never inferred from selling prices or silently backfilled from current costs. Allow audited historical order-cost entry/correction. Snapshot new orders in POS, online checkout and admin add/swap; later product edits do not change past costs. Legacy paid timestamps use first successful payment evidence or order creation with an explicit estimate marker. Paid POS creation is exact. Capture future gateway settlement and COD collection atomically with existing status updates. Refunded orders lock financial item/price edits while cost metadata remains editable.

Add /admin/sales plus existing dashboard/sidebar links. Show today/week/month/custom dates, paid/pending/delivered counts, revenue, discounts, recorded refunds, net sales and gross profit/cost coverage; separate POS/online totals; daily summaries, payment/product/collection/size breakdowns, best sellers and shared low-stock material alerts. Reuse KpiTile and inventory thresholds. Poll only while the sales page is visible every 15 seconds, abort obsolete requests, show timestamps/errors and manual refresh. Cache aggregate results briefly, deduplicate concurrent reads, cap query sizes (5,000 paid orders / 20,000 lines / 2,000 refunds) and custom ranges to 366 days, and ask users to narrow an oversized report rather than show incomplete figures. Use read-only consistent transactions with timeouts and existing/new date indexes.

CSV and real XLSX exports include summaries/daily/channel/payment/product/collection/size data without customer contact details. Encode spreadsheet strings as text, guard CSV formulas, and use bounded native ZIP/OpenXML generation without installing large libraries. Test cents, allocation, duplicates, paid dates, refunds/returns, cancelled orders, missing costs, date boundaries, authorization, concurrency, read limits, exports, automatic refresh and existing POS/checkout flows. Use isolated PostgreSQL/browser fixtures, a private Prisma client, sequential capped checks/builds, compatible bounded-lock migration, fresh production generation, private canary and app-only release with rollback. No production test orders or messages.
