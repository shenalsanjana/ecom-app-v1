# Customized design orders through Business WhatsApp

Admins can mark products as Customized Design Product on create/edit. The existing custom canvas tote and cotton canvas pouch are explicitly enabled by an additive migration. Product pages and checkout explain PNG submission through Business WhatsApp (+94 74 054 5536), keep purchase available, and prefill the product names and canonical links. Order confirmation and account history include an order-number-prefilled WhatsApp button. The release also aligns the two existing product descriptions with purchase-now/submit-PNG-after-checkout instructions and customer approval of any extra printing charges, using backed-up compare-and-update writes on those description fields only.

The product flag is copied to order lines in online checkout, POS and admin add/swap operations. Later product changes do not remove the saved requirement. Per-item designReceivedAt records admin-confirmed receipt; opening WhatsApp never claims a file was sent. Pending reminders list the remaining items until every required design is received. Admin controls are authenticated, order-scoped, version-checked, reversible and audited in internal notes. No WhatsApp messages are sent automatically.

Custom Design is another product design within each existing physical family and storefront subcategory. The two products already had the correct primary collections. Exactly two redundant additional memberships and the now-empty Custom Design Orders collection were removed through a guarded data correction and the existing admin deletion action, which invalidates catalog caches. Live verification confirms all 21 accessory products remain under three subcategories, with each custom product present in the appropriate tote/pouch page. Product IDs, names, prices, photos and stock bindings remain intact. The historical one-off importer is retired locally to prevent recreating the obsolete collection.

Validation passed: 1,290 unit tests, 21 isolated PostgreSQL integration tests, scoped changed-source TypeScript, ESLint, a memory/CPU-bounded two-stage production build and three browser flows. Browser coverage includes admin create/edit persistence, custom and regular product pages, a guest restored mixed cart with COD confirmation, order references in WhatsApp links, a WhatsApp click retaining pending status, per-item admin receipt/reset, prepaid confirmation, customer history, admin access protection and mobile layout. All browser requests to external sites are blocked; no production orders or WhatsApp messages are submitted.

The low-memory build path limits webpack to two concurrent transforms. Resource limits and host memory guards protect production during checks. The release applies only defaulted metadata columns with bounded SQL lock/statement timeouts, regenerates pages after removing isolated-test caches, checks a private canary and activates only the app with rollback retained. A targeted metadata catch-up after activation covers any order lines the old app saved for the two existing custom products during the compatible migration/cutover window. PostgreSQL and nginx are not recreated during deployment; nginx is reloaded after activation. Read-only live checks verify both product notices, admin flags, original subcategories, regular-product behavior and POS photo decoding. Release and interruption evidence is retained under tmp/custom-design-release/.
