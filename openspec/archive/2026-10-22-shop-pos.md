# Shop POS integration — 2026-10-22

Added an admin-only in-store register backed by the existing catalogue, order
history and shared physical/print inventory. Cash sales calculate change in
integer LKR cents; external card terminal sales record the approved reference.
Paid, collected sales receive sequence-backed POS receipt numbers and can be
searched and reprinted from Orders.

Serializable transactions, canonical intent hashes and register-tab recovery
protect against duplicate sales and shared-stock overselling. The migration is
additive and defaults existing orders to the ONLINE sales channel.

Validation: 1,254 unit tests, 9 isolated PostgreSQL tests, 5 Chromium cashier
flows, 30 category/PayHere regression tests, production webpack build,
TypeScript and lint. Temporary files are excluded from Docker build context.
