# Unified POS and online sales dashboard

Admin → Sales dashboard now reports settled sales from the existing orders database by payment received date in Asia/Colombo, as confirmed by the user. Today, Monday-based week, calendar month and inclusive custom ranges show revenue, merchandise net sales, order count, discounts, verified refunds and gross profit; POS/online totals; daily summaries and a chart; payment, product, category/collection and size breakdowns; best sellers; and physical shared-material stock alerts. Existing operational dashboard cards and POS controls remain available.

A visible page refreshes every 15 seconds and suspends polling in hidden tabs. Short cached aggregates, one computation at a time, read-only repeatable-read transactions, query timeouts, date indexes and explicit limits protect checkout and inventory writes. Reports exceeding 366 days, 5,000 paid orders, 2,000 refund records or 20,000 combined lines request a narrower range instead of displaying partial totals. CSV and real seven-sheet XLSX exports share the report engine, carry no customer contact details, protect spreadsheet text from formula execution and require admin access.

New order lines snapshot internal unit cost, list price, product and collection identity. POS settlement, verified gateway success and COD collection capture the first receipt timestamp without resetting it for retries. Legacy gateway evidence backfills exact dates; other legacy dates are labelled estimates. No cost is inferred from a selling price, and no old markdown or free-text refund amount is invented. Missing costs leave gross profit unavailable, with links to the relevant orders. Product editing sets costs for future sales; historical order cost corrections are explicitly audited. These internal values are not exposed on product or customer confirmation pages.

Order Sales accounting records refunds already completed and verified with their provider. References, dates, reasons, per-line merchandise/delivery credits and optional resellable quantities already returned to stock are audited. Idempotency, unique active references, serializable transactions, version claims and remaining-value/quantity checks prevent duplicate or excessive credits, including concurrent different refund attempts. Voiding preserves history and permits a corrected reference. Refund recording and correction do not send money, dispatch orders or alter stock. Cancelled orders are excluded from sales; their refunds remain separately visible without double deduction. Gross profit excludes delivery, deducts saved product costs and recovers only explicitly recorded resellable return costs.

Validation passed: 1,320 unit tests; 32 tests against disposable PostgreSQL covering POS/online/COD snapshots, first settlement dates, duplicate callbacks, sales reconciliation, concurrent refunds, caps, corrections, authorisation and oversized ranges; a seeded pre-migration database verifying legacy date/category/SKU backfill; scoped TypeScript including new test fixtures; ESLint; the capped Next production build; four desktop/mobile browser flows covering report filters, polling, stale-data recovery, protected downloads, cost entry, refund recording/voiding and an existing POS cash sale/receipt. Downloaded XLSX ZIP checksums/XML, all seven sheets, numeric totals and unknown-profit blanks were independently checked, as were matching CSV totals.

Work followed the repository's documented design/plan/proposal/worktree/validation/archive workflow manually because the named Superpowers/OpenSpec command skills are not available in this session. The reporting delta specification was synced into openspec/specs/sales-reporting/. Checks run sequentially with CPU/memory caps and a host memory reserve. Release preparation retains the old app image, applies only additive schema changes with short lock/statement timeouts, removes isolated test caches, regenerates pages using production data, verifies a private canary and activates only the app. The database and proxy are not recreated. Final runtime, boot/container identity and read-only sales/health checks are recorded under tmp/sales-dashboard-release/; no live test orders, payments, refunds or cost entries are created.
